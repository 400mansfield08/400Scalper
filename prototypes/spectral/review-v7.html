What's new in v7

  • In Spectral mode the display now sits just left of OUT. The middle panel (the band editor in Sample mode) is hidden, so the bottom row reads IN · THRESHOLD · KNEE · DETAIL · CURVE · SPECTRUM · OUT.
  • The display is a live frequency spectrum. Accent color = what's being clipped right now. The opposite color (blue against the default orange) = detail being kept by the DETAIL knob. Grey = passing through untouched.
  • The "kept" color is always the opposite hue of the accent, so it updates if you change the accent color. The DETAIL knob uses the same color, so it visibly matches what it controls.
  • Turn DETAIL down and the kept color fades to grey; push IN harder and more of the low end lights up in the accent color.

From v6 — Spectral mode (prototype)

  • Click SPECTRAL at the top. The bottom row switches to Spectral mode: it gets an accent line, a SPECTRAL MODE tag, and the band editor is replaced by the ear filter bank.
  • (v6 only) The ear filter bank showed 26 bands spaced the way the ear hears pitch. Accent-colored bands are the loud content being clipped. Light bands are quiet detail that passes through untouched.
  • DETAIL (new knob) sets how much of that detail is protected. At 0% it behaves like the normal clipper. Turn it up and watch the light bands; on the scope, the waveform keeps its texture through the clipped peaks.
  • Spectral mode clips the whole signal, so there's no band splitting, solo, or crossover slope. THRESHOLD and KNEE work as usual, and nothing passes the threshold.
  • This preview runs the real prototype algorithm. In the plugin it adds about 11 ms of delay (12 ms in total), which the DAW compensates for.

From v5

  • Accent color. Click 400SCALPER at the top left to open a color wheel. Pick any accent color, adjust its brightness, or type a hex code. RESET goes back to the default TPL orange.

From v4

  • Solo. Each band has a small S button at its top right. Turn on one or more to hear only those bands. Bands that aren't soloed dim in the spectrum, and the OUT meter shows only the soloed bands.
  • Crossover frequency tags moved down slightly so they don't sit under the S buttons.

From v3

  • Multiband (optional). The panel between IN and THRESHOLD shows a live spectrum. With one band (the default) it's a normal clipper.
  • To add a band, drag in from the ‹ or › tab on the left or right edge, or right-click / long-press inside the spectrum. Up to 4 bands.
  • Drag a crossover line to move it. Double-click it, or right-click it, to remove it.
  • Click a band to select it. THRESHOLD and KNEE then set that band, and the display shows that band's clipping.
  • 6 / 12 / 24 dB sets the crossover slope.
  • FOLLOW is now the other way round. On (the default): the display scrolls with the playhead. Off: the display holds still and a playhead sweeps across it.

Still here from v2

  • Drag a knob up or down; double-click or double-tap to reset. Drag the dashed threshold line on the display.
  • The orange input sits behind the grey output, so orange only shows where the clipper cut the signal.
  • TIME and ZOOM sliders, FREEZE, and FIT. IN and OUT meters on either side. SPECTRAL is a placeholder.
